## Description

```
A short read and a gentle daily planner — for students, people starting their first job, and anyone who feels overwhelmed by detailed planning systems.

Instead of a big system, three small ideas:
• put what's on your mind somewhere outside your head,
• choose one main task for today and its first small step,
• change the plan when the day changes.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
This is a DIGITAL product. Nothing will be shipped.

1) The web app (opens from a private link in your browser)
• READ — a complete short book: introduction, three chapters and a closing note, with original illustrations, everyday scenes and one small exercise per chapter. Research findings are summarised with their limits and sources, kept separate from advice.
• MY DAY — one main task and its first small step, an optional start time ("when and where"), at most two extra tasks and an optional evening check-in. A monthly calendar shows your past days. Empty days are simply empty — no scores.
• MY HABIT — one small habit, set up in six short questions and tried for seven days. Each day: "I did my small step", "I did my smaller option" or "Not today", plus an optional note. No points, no badges, no streak counters.

2) The book as a PDF — in English, plus Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Dutch (zip).

3) A "Start here" PDF with your app link, a QR code and step-by-step instructions for iPhone, Android and computer.
```
